03 Digital application

Consistent UI across every subdomain

Navigation

One global top navigation bar, one responsive menu, one theme-aware logo and a visible sign-in action.

Course cards

White or off-white surfaces, graphite text, blue metadata and orange only for deadlines.

Accessibility

Visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, WCAG AA text contrast and 44px minimum interactive targets.

04 Social media

Channel rules that preserve recognition

LinkedIn and Facebook

Use bold short titles, one proof point, one CTA and stacked or hero lockups.

Instagram and YouTube

Protect negative space for overlays, subtitles and platform-safe crops.

WhatsApp

Use square stacked lockups, larger mobile type and minimal copy.

Do not: place partner marks inside the Academy logo, use unapproved effects, publish stretched marks, or fill posts with dense body text.

Academy-led material

Skunkworks Academy is primary. Partner marks are secondary, separated by clearspace or a neutral divider.

Joint material

Use optically balanced logo size, preserve each party’s exclusion zone and never create a merged hybrid mark.

06 Terms of use

Approved use is controlled and revocable

Permitted

Internal Academy communications, approved courseware, authorised partner campaigns, proposals and learner-facing platforms.

Prohibited

Alteration, resale, sublicensing, false endorsement, deceptive association, political use, unlawful content or use that harms the brand.

Approval

Third-party and co-branded publication requires written approval from Skunkworks Academy before release.

Ownership

All marks, templates, tokens and derivative assets remain the property of their respective rights holders.

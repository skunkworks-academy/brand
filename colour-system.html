06 · Colour system

One canonical palette for every Academy design.

Use these exact values. Do not sample colours manually from screenshots or compressed exports. Skunk Blue remains the canonical blue; the lighter blue-on-dark token is an accessibility support value for small text on Ink Navy.

Ink Navy

#03033A
RGB 3, 3, 58

Premium dark backgrounds, hero shells, dark LMS and portal surfaces.

Skunk Blue

#1E6BD0
RGB 30, 107, 208

Primary digital accent, CTAs, links, active states and blue logo use on dark.

Signal Orange

#F24208
RGB 242, 66, 8

Restrained accent for dividers, urgency, campaign energy and emphasis rules.

White

#FFFFFF
RGB 255, 255, 255

Negative space, reversed marks, document and course-content surfaces.
